How to use AI Academic Agent

From a source document to a correctly formatted citation in 8 steps. 2 free analyses every day.

Try it free

8 steps

  1. Create an accountSign up with your email and confirm it. You get 2 free analyses every day.
  2. Paste your abstractEnter your abstract or research proposal. It is the yardstick every source is scored against.
  3. Upload a documentChoose a PDF, DOC or DOCX file (up to 5 MB, or 15 MB for accounts verified by an administrator). Scans are read with OCR.
  4. Read the fit scoreGet a score from 0 to 100 on five criteria (topic, concepts, method, evidence, currency), with a summary and comments.
  5. Pick passages worth citingAt 60 or above, the tool suggests passages to quote, ranked by priority, with page numbers, each checked verbatim against the source.
  6. Copy the citationChoose APA, Harvard, Chicago, MLA, IEEE, Vancouver, AMA, BibTeX, RIS or over 10,000 journal styles (CSL), then copy the reference and the in-text citation.
  7. Review history by topicCopied citations are grouped by topic; switch the style and language right on each source.
  8. Low score? Choose a pathBelow 60, the tool suggests other sources, a sharper abstract and suitable journals from EduFind. You decide the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What is AI Academic Agent?

AI Academic Agent is a bilingual Vietnamese/English web app that helps researchers read sources, judge how well they fit a study, find passages worth citing and create citations to international standards.

Is it free?

Each account gets 2 free analyses per day. To request more, contact the administrator.

Are my documents stored?

No. Files are read on your device; only the extracted text is sent for analysis and it is not stored. The system keeps only the citations you copied and the topics you chose to save.

Which formats and sizes are accepted?

PDF, DOC and DOCX, up to 5 MB, or 15 MB for accounts verified by an administrator. Scans are read with OCR.

How is the fit score calculated?

A 100-point scale over five criteria: topic (40), concepts and theory (20), method (15), evidence (15) and currency (10). A score of 60 or more passes the citation threshold.

Can it invent quotes?

Every suggested passage is checked verbatim against the source text, and any that does not match is dropped. You should still read the source before relying on it.

Which citation styles are supported?

APA 7, Harvard, Chicago 17, MLA 9, IEEE, Vancouver, AMA 11, BibTeX, RIS and over 10,000 CSL journal and university styles, in Vietnamese or English.

What happens below 60?

No passages are suggested. Instead the app points you to alternative sources and suitable journals for your field from EduFind.
