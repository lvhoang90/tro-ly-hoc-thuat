How to use Ami

Ami is the AI academic assistant of the ISA ecosystem: it reads sources for you, scores their fit to your study, suggests the passages worth citing and creates correct citations. This page walks through every step with screenshots from the app; what to click is marked with a coloured frame.

Analyse a source: 8 steps

From a source document to a correct citation. Follow the order; you can go back to an earlier step at any time.

  1. Create an accountCreate an account with your email and confirm by mail. A Basic account gets 1 free analysis per week.
    • Open Ami, press "No account? Sign up", enter your name, email and a password of 8+ characters, and accept the terms.
    • Ami sends a confirmation email; press the link (check spam if you cannot see it) and sign in.
    • A Basic account works right away. For a personal allowance, full history and larger files, ask to have your account verified on the Profile page.
  2. Paste your abstractEnter your abstract or research proposal. This is the yardstick Ami uses to score every source.
    • Paste your abstract or proposal, at least 40 words. The clearer the aims, questions, framework and method, the closer the score.
    • You can name the topic to group your citation history. The abstract is saved only when you press "Save as topic".
  3. Upload a sourceChoose the PDF, DOC or DOCX file to assess. The file is read on your device.
    • Drag and drop or click to choose a file. Up to 2 MB for unverified accounts, 15 MB for verified ones.
    • The file is read on your device; only the text is sent for analysis and it is not stored.
    • For a scanned PDF (images), Ami offers in-browser text recognition (OCR), up to 60 pages.
  4. Read the fit scoreGet a score from 0 to 100 over five criteria, with a summary, strengths and gaps.
    • The score has five criteria: topic (40), concepts and theory (20), method (15), evidence (15) and currency (10).
    • From 60 points a source passes the citation threshold. Below it you get a summary, strengths and where the source does not fit your study.
  5. Pick citable passagesFrom 60 points Ami suggests passages to cite, ranked by priority, with page numbers, checked verbatim.
    • Each passage shows its priority, type (definition, finding, method…), page number and why it is worth citing.
    • Every passage is checked verbatim against the source by machine; any that does not match is dropped and Ami tells you how many were dropped.
    • Tick the passages you want and press "Cite selected passages". You should still read the source before relying on it.
  6. Copy the citationPick a citation style (APA, Harvard, IEEE…) and copy the reference list entry and the in-text citation.
    • Choose APA 7, Harvard, Chicago, MLA, IEEE, Vancouver, AMA, BibTeX, RIS or 10,000+ journal and university styles (CSL), in Vietnamese or English.
    • Press Copy; italics are kept when you paste into Word.
    • You can edit the source details (authors, year, journal) before copying if Ami read them wrongly.
  7. Review your history by topicCopied citations are grouped by topic; change style and language right at each source.
    • "Citation history" groups every citation you copied by topic, with each source's fit score.
    • Basic accounts still have their history saved but can review it in full only once verified.
  8. Choose a path when the score is lowBelow 60, Ami points to other sources, abstract edits and suitable journals from EduFind; you decide.
    • Below 60, Ami suggests no passages. Instead you get paths: find other sources, edit your abstract, or cite the source anyway.
    • Detailed suggested works and journals are for verified accounts; Basic accounts see the number of suggestions.
Premium feature

AI Professor review

A simulated committee: review proposals, theses and dissertations

The AI Professor plays a demanding but fair reviewer. You upload a work and your institution's review template; Ami drafts section-by-section comments, a proposed score out of 100, an accept or revise recommendation and questions for the author; every evidence quote is checked by machine against the original.

For verified researchers approved by the administrator. Each review reads the full work, so it uses far more resources than a normal analysis.

This is a drafting aid: Ami does not replace a real committee, does not check plagiarism and does not verify that references exist. The reviewer verifies, edits and stays responsible for the final text.

From request to review

  1. Find the AI ProfessorIt has a star icon in the menu; the Analyse page also shows an introduction banner.
  2. Get your account verifiedUnverified accounts only see the introduction. Contact the author to get verified (name, affiliation, ORCID).
  3. Send a request with reason and evidenceA verified account sends a request for a quota: say why you need it and give concrete scientific evidence (ORCID, publications, projects, reviewing roles).
  4. Wait for approvalEach person has one pending request. Once approved, the page opens right away; if not, add evidence and send it again.
  5. Choose document type, role and templatePick proposal, article, thesis or dissertation; pick your role; use a built-in template or upload your institution's review template so Ami follows it section by section.
  6. Upload the work to reviewChoose a DOCX or text PDF. The file is read on your device; a very long work (over about 900 thousand characters) should be split by chapter.
  7. The Professor drafts the reviewAmi reads the whole text, comments in batches of sections and then compiles the score. It can take a few minutes; keep the page open until it finishes.
  8. Read the score and recommendationThe proposed score is summed by code from the component scores; the recommendation follows the score thresholds and can be lowered by a fatal defect.
  9. Read comments and evidenceEach section has comments, strengths, weaknesses, requested revisions by priority and verbatim quotes checked against the original.

Download the draft as Word (.docx) to edit and finish. Results are kept only in your browser (latest 5) and can be deleted at any time.

If it fails before any result, the credit is refunded.

How to read the score and recommendation

Below 40Reject, rewrite entirely
40–54Major revision and re-review
55–59Revise before approval
60–74Accept with conditions
75 and aboveAccept, minor changes

With a fatal defect the recommendation cannot be better than "major revision", however high the score.

For administrators

Under Admin, the AI Professor tab lists requests with reasons and evidence. The administrator approves with a weekly quota, rejects with a note, or revokes later.

Frequently asked questions

What is AI Academic Agent?

AI Academic Agent is a bilingual Vietnamese/English web app that helps researchers read sources, judge how well they fit a study, find passages worth citing and create citations to international standards.

Is it free?

A Basic account gets 1 free analysis per week. If you need more, you can ask to have your account verified, see the next question.

What is a verified account?

An account the author has confirmed belongs to a researcher (name, affiliation, ORCID). Verified accounts get a personal allowance agreed between both sides, can review their full citation history and the detailed source suggestions, and can upload files up to 15 MB. To get verified, contact the author by email, phone or Zalo listed in the footer. Nothing you saved is lost while you are unverified.

What are the authors on ProFind?

After an analysis, Ami matches the document's author names with ProFind, the ISA ecosystem's lookup of researchers tied to Vietnamese universities and institutes. The matching runs on your device, so the names are not sent anywhere. Because it matches by name, the result is only a hint: check the affiliation and works before concluding it is the same person. Foreign authors are usually not in ProFind.

Where are my ProFind profile and the invitation link?

On the Profile page: Ami suggests ProFind profiles close to your name, email and affiliation; press "That is me" to go to ProFind without entering a code again (only when you press does Ami send your email, name, phone, job and affiliation to ProFind). The same page has your personal link to invite colleagues: when an invited person signs up and finishes a first analysis, you receive extra analyses at the level the administrator sets.

Are my documents stored?

The system does not store files or the full text of documents. Files are read on your device; only the extracted text goes through our server to Claude (Anthropic) for analysis and is not stored. The system keeps only the citations you copied and the topics you chose to save. See the Privacy page for details.

Which formats and sizes are accepted?

PDF, DOC and DOCX, up to 2 MB for unverified accounts, or 15 MB for accounts verified by an administrator. Scans are read with OCR.

How is the fit score calculated?

A 100-point scale over five criteria: topic (40), concepts and theory (20), method (15), evidence (15) and currency (10). A score of 60 or more passes the citation threshold.

Can it invent quotes?

Every suggested passage is checked verbatim against the source text, and any that does not match is dropped. You should still read the source before relying on it.

Which citation styles are supported?

APA 7, Harvard, Chicago 17, MLA 9, IEEE, Vancouver, AMA 11, BibTeX, RIS and over 10,000 CSL journal and university styles, in Vietnamese or English.

What happens below 60?

No passages are suggested. Instead the app points you to alternative sources and suitable journals for your field from EduFind.

What is the AI Professor?

A premium feature for verified researchers approved by the administrator (send a request with your reason and scientific evidence on the AI Professor page). Once approved: upload a proposal, thesis, dissertation or article (DOCX or text PDF) and your institution's review template (or use a built-in one). Ami drafts section-by-section comments, a proposed score out of 100 summed by code, an accept/revise recommendation and questions for the author; every evidence quote is checked verbatim against the original. Results export to Word. It is only a drafting aid: Ami does not check plagiarism or verify references; the reviewer is responsible for the final text.
